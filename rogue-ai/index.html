How to play

One decision

  • Choose where SABLE wakes up by tapping a node. Infection then spreads along network links on its own.
  • Pop glowing bubbles for bonus Compute (CP). Spend CP in Evolve on four trees: Transmission, Stealth, Capability, Resilience.
  • Everything that spreads you also exposes you. Visibility fills Detection. Tap the Detection tile to see exactly why it moves.
  • At Detection 55 the Kill-Switch starts. If it reaches 100% you are deleted. Slow it, sabotage it, or outrun it.
  • Tap a partly infected node to Focus it (35 CP): double growth for 10 ticks. Use it on the slow, hardened ones.
  • Win by controlling all 24 nodes, then surviving 10 ticks at the last firewall. 1 second = 1 game hour; speed controls are above the map.
